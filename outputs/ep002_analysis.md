# AI in Healthcare: Hype or Reality?

## Summary

In this episode of Tech Health Today, David Chen discusses AI applications in healthcare with Dr. Priya Patel and Alex Moore. The conversation covers the use of AI for diagnostics and triage assistants, regulatory hurdles, data privacy and bias concerns, and future trends such as predictive diagnostics and interoperability.

## Top 5 Takeaways

- AI is being used assistively in hospitals for reading X-rays and flagging urgent cases.
- Regulatory barriers prevent full autonomy of AI systems in healthcare.
- Hospitals are adopting AI to improve efficiency and clinician support.
- Data privacy and bias are significant challenges for AI in healthcare.
- Predictive diagnostics using AI is a future trend, but with human oversight.

## Notable Quotes

> **01:50 — Dr. Priya:** Smart triage chatbots on hospital websites or apps, helping patients figure out next steps before seeing a doctor.
> **02:50 — Alex:** Data privacy and bias are major hurdles. AI learns from existing clinical data, which may be skewed.
